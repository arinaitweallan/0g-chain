# Code of Conduct

## Purpose
The purpose of this Code of Conduct is to ensure a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for all contributors and users of 0G (Zero Gravity). Everyone involved is expected to follow these guidelines to maintain a welcoming and collaborative space.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all contributors, maintainers, and users participating in any aspect of the project, including code contributions, discussions, issue reporting, documentation, and interactions in online and offline spaces.

## Expected Behavior
- Treat everyone with respect and professionalism.
- Provide constructive feedback and be open to receiving it.
- Use inclusive and welcoming language.
- Respect different opinions, experiences, and backgrounds.
- Collaborate in a way that fosters a positive environment.
- Ensure communication remains clear, polite, and relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive comments based on personal characteristics such as race, gender, religion, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or spreading false information.
- Threats, intimidation, or acts of violence.
- Posting private or sensitive information without consent.
- Intentional disruption of discussions or project activities.
- Plagiarism or failure to properly attribute contributions.

## Reporting Violations
If you experience or witness any violation of this Code of Conduct, you are encouraged to report it to the project maintainers. Reports should include relevant details and evidence if available. All reports will be reviewed and handled with confidentiality and seriousness.

## Consequences of Violations
Violations of this Code of Conduct may result in actions such as:
- A formal warning.
- Temporary or permanent suspension from the project.
- Removal of contributions or privileges.
- Legal action in cases of severe misconduct.

## Acknowledgment
By participating in this project, you agree to adhere to this Code of Conduct and contribute to a respectful and positive community.

## Contact Information
For reporting concerns or seeking clarification, contact the project maintainers at their [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/0glabs).
